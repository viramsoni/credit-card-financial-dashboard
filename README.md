## 💼 Business Problem
Financial institutions manage millions of daily credit card transactions. Without structured visualization, operations managers cannot track active customer spend velocity, revenue generation metrics, or detect early patterns of customer credit defaults.

## 🛠️ Data & Analytical Logic
This interactive Power BI dashboard was built to analyze financial performance metrics by combining multiple relational transaction tables.

*   **Data Cleaning:** Used Power Query/Pandas logic to filter anomalies, remove duplicated records, and handle empty text values cleanly.
*   **Data Modeling:** Established relationships between Customer Demographic tables and Monthly Transaction ledgers.
*   **Core Business Insights Tracked:**
    *   **Total Revenue & Interest Earned:** Monitors the overall profitability generated from monthly customer balances.
    *   **Customer Delinquency/Default Rate:** Flags high-risk account brackets to reduce bad debt write-offs.
    *   **Transaction Volume by Category:** Identifies where customers are spending most (e.g., Bills, Entertainment, Travel) to optimize targeted marketing campaigns.

## 🚀 Key Business Impact
This visualization allows finance operations teams to transition away from messy manual Excel sheets and monitor financial performance via a single, automated operational dashboard.
